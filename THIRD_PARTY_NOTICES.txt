4. Card reference data (tcgsuite/seed/reference/)
======================================================================
The reference pack lists card names, set names, collector numbers, languages and
picture addresses so the program can recognise your cards. It was compiled from
public sources:

  - Pokemon TCG data   github.com/PokemonTCG/pokemon-tcg-data (community data)
  - TCGdex             tcgdex.net (open card data in several languages)
  - Riftbound          Riot Games' public card gallery (riftbound.leagueoflegends.com)
  - One Piece          Bandai's public card list (en.onepiece-cardgame.com)
  - Cardmarket         the public product-catalog and price-guide files that
                       Cardmarket publishes for download (prices and product ids)

Riftbound decklists (Bulk lots > Read popular decks) are read on demand from the
public pages of riftbound.zone; nothing from that site is bundled.
Card pictures are not bundled: they are downloaded on your computer, one by one,
from the sources above, and kept as small thumbnails.
